Turning this into a real video

01 · CAPTURE

Screen-record the demo

Play it full-screen and record at 1080p/60 with OBS or your OS recorder. This piece is the storyboard and the footage in one — no separate mockups to build.

02 · VOICE

Generate the narration

Feed the script below to ElevenLabs (~$5) for a natural read, or record a human voiceover. One take per scene keeps editing simple.

03 · ASSEMBLE

Lay VO over footage

In Descript or CapCut: drop the voice track under the recording, nudge scene timings to match, add quiet background music, export MP4. Half a day.

Avoid Runway / Sora / Veo for the interface shots — generative video can't reproduce an accurate UI walkthrough. Use them only for an optional atmospheric opening or closing shot.

Narration script — 12 scenes, ~3:05
  1. Scene 1 · The problemAisha Rao built FocusGarden over three weekends, with an AI writing most of the code. It compiles, it runs, it's genuinely good — and it is going nowhere.
  2. Scene 2 · The wallPublishing, monetization, billing, operations, growth — five different disciplines, each weeks of unfamiliar work. This is where almost every solo builder stops.
  3. Scene 3 · Hand it overSo she hands the whole thing to LaunchPad. One form — the repo, the name, the category, how she wants to charge. That's the last complicated decision she has to make.
  4. Scene 4 · The paperworkFirst, the boring fortnight — entity, D-U-N-S number, a Play account, Apple enrolment, bank and tax forms — done for her or walked through step by step. She also authorises the revenue-share mandate: LaunchPad only gets paid when she does.
  5. Scene 5 · Build & signThe app is pulled straight from her repo, configured, and signed with a key held in a managed vault — so she never babysits a certificate again. Build one: green.
  6. Scene 6 · Test & auto-fixForty-seven checks: crashes, performance, and every store-policy rule. Three problems turn up — and all three are fixed automatically, then the suite re-runs clean. This is why the app won't bounce back from review.
  7. Scene 7 · MonetizeNow the money model. A seven-day free trial, three hundred and ninety-nine rupees a month or two thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine a year, with local pricing filled in for every market. The paywall is built, previewed, and wired to entitlements — no SDK glue.
  8. Scene 8 · SubmitLaunchPad generates the store listing — title, keywords, screenshots in device frames, a preview video — and submits to both stores under Aisha's own accounts. Then everyone waits.
  9. Scene 9 · LiveGoogle clears it in eight hours. Apple, in a day. FocusGarden is live on both stores — and Aisha hasn't opened a store console once.
  10. Scene 10 · RevenueInstalls come in. Trials start. Subscriptions convert. Six weeks later FocusGarden is at forty-seven thousand rupees of monthly recurring revenue. The store pays Aisha; LaunchPad invoices its fifteen percent against reconciled reports, automatically.
  11. Scene 11 · GrowthThen the growth engine goes to work. A better paywall wins a test and ships to everyone. App-store keywords climb. A small ad campaign pays itself back in ten weeks. The line steepens.
  12. Scene 12 · CloseAisha's job is now exactly one thing: make FocusGarden better. Everything after the build runs itself.
